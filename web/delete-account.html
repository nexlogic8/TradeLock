Delete your TradeLock account

TradeLock is operated by Nexlogic LLP, India. This page applies to the TradeLock website and the TradeLock Android app (in.tradelock.app).

1. Delete it yourself (instant)

  1. Sign in at tradelock.in/login or in the TradeLock app.
  2. Open Profile (tap your picture at the top right in the app).
  3. Scroll to Delete account and tap Delete account.
  4. Type DELETE (and your authenticator code if two-step sign-in is on) and confirm.

Your account is deleted immediately and you are signed out on every device.

2. Or ask us by email

Can't sign in? Email support@nexlogic.in from the email address of your TradeLock account with the subject "Delete my TradeLock account". We verify the request and delete the account within 7 days.

3. What is deleted

  • Your name, email address, phone number and profile picture.
  • Your broker connection and its encrypted access token.
  • Your risk rules, P&L history, positions and trade records, P&L calendar and analytics.
  • Alerts, notifications, settings, sign-in sessions and your referral code.

4. What is kept, and for how long

  • Payment, invoice and wallet records (amounts, dates, GST): kept for 8 years as required by Indian tax law (CGST Act), linked only to an anonymous "Deleted user", not to your name or email.
  • Security and fraud-prevention records (a scrambled device identifier and an anonymous log of account actions): kept for up to 2 years to prevent misuse such as repeated free trials.

Any unused wallet balance and any remaining subscription time end when the account is deleted, so withdraw your wallet balance first. Payments are handled by Razorpay, which keeps its own records under its privacy policy.

Email support@nexlogic.in. See also our Privacy Policy.
