Refund & Cancellation Policy

Effective 10 October 2026 · TradeLock is operated by Nexlogic LLP, India.

2. Cancelling your subscription

  • There is no automatic debit. Each month is paid by you from Subscription in the app; to stop, simply do not pay for the next month.
  • After cancelling you keep full access until the end of the month you have already paid for. You will not be charged again.

3. Refunds

  • Duplicate or failed payments: if you were charged twice, or money left your account but the purchase did not complete, we refund the full amount.
  • Service unavailable: if TradeLock was unavailable for more than 48 hours in a row during a paid month because of a fault on our side, we refund that month on request.
  • First paid month: if you are not satisfied, ask within 7 days of your first payment and we refund it in full.
  • Otherwise, subscription months that have started are not refunded, and nothing further is charged unless you choose to pay for another month.

4. Wallet balance

  • Added funds can be used only to pay for TradeLock and cannot be transferred or withdrawn as cash, as required for closed-system wallets in India.
  • If you close your account, any unused added funds are refunded to the original payment method on request.
  • Referral earnings can be withdrawn to your UPI ID or bank account from the Wallet page.

5. How refunds are paid

Approved refunds go back to the original payment method (UPI, card or netbanking) within 5–7 working days of approval. Your bank may take a few additional days to show it.

6. How to request a refund

Email support@nexlogic.in from your registered email address with the payment date, amount and reason. We reply within 2 working days.

TradeLock is a discipline and risk-monitoring tool. Trading losses are not refundable and are not our responsibility; see the Terms of Service.
