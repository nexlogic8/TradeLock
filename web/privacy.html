Privacy Policy

Effective 10 October 2026 · TradeLock is operated by Nexlogic LLP, India.

This policy explains what data TradeLock (“we”, “us”) collects when you use tradelock.in, why we collect it, and the choices you have. We follow India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

1. Data we collect

From Google sign-in we request only your name, email address and basic profile (scopes openid, email, profile). We do not access your Gmail, Drive, contacts or any other Google data.

2. How we use it

  • To provide TradeLock: monitoring, rule checks, alerts, order gating and emergency actions.
  • To secure accounts, prevent abuse and keep an audit record of sensitive actions.
  • To send service messages such as risk alerts, broker connection issues and billing notices.

We do not sell your data, do not use it for advertising, and do not use it to train AI models. We do not trade on your behalf except for the specific actions you configure (for example, an exit when your daily loss limit is reached).

3. Who we share it with

  • Your broker (e.g. Dhan): only the API requests needed to read your account and carry out your configured actions.
  • Amazon Web Services (Mumbai region): hosting and storage.
  • Google: only if you use Google sign-in.
  • Hostinger: our email provider, for messages we send you.
  • Authorities, when required by Indian law.

Google user data received through Google sign-in is used and transferred in line with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

4. Cookies

We use only essential cookies: a session cookie that keeps you signed in, a short-lived cookie that protects Google sign-in, and a referral cookie for 30 days if you arrive through a referral link. We use no analytics or advertising cookies.

5. Security

All traffic uses HTTPS. Passwords are hashed, broker tokens are encrypted at rest, sessions are stored as hashes, and sensitive actions can require an authenticator-app code. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affects you we will notify you and the Data Protection Board of India as the law requires.

6. Retention

We keep your account data while your account is active. When you delete your account (Profile → Delete account, or see how to delete your account), your personal and trading data is erased at once. Billing and wallet records are kept, without your name, for 8 years as tax law requires, and security records (such as a scrambled device identifier used to allow one free trial per person) for up to 2 years.

7. Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct or delete your data, withdraw consent, or nominate someone to act for you. Disconnecting your broker in the app deletes your stored broker token immediately. To make any request, email support@nexlogic.in. We reply within 30 days.

8. Children

TradeLock is for adults trading their own accounts. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18.

9. Changes

If we change this policy in a meaningful way, we will notify you in the app or by email before the change takes effect.
