What you get

  • A fast native app: Overview, Power Scalper, AI Algo Trading, Risk rules, P&L calendar, Journal, Analytics, Wallet and Broker accounts, built for the phone with smooth live P&L. Anything not yet native opens from tradelock.in inside the app.

How to install

  1. On your Android phone, tap Download APK above.
  2. Open the downloaded file (from the notification or your Downloads folder).
  3. If Android asks, allow Install unknown apps for your browser, then go back and tap Install. The app is not on the Play Store yet, so Android shows this warning for any app installed from a website.
  4. Open TradeLock and sign in with your tradelock.in email and password (or Google).
  5. When asked, allow Notifications, Alarms & reminders and Display over other apps so trade alarms can ring.

Updating

When a new app version is released, download it from this page and install it over the old one; your sign-in and settings stay. Version 2.1 installs over the earlier 1.4 app the same way.
