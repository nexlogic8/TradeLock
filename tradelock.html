What it does

Your rules, checked on every sync

Set your limits before the market opens. TradeLock watches your positions and P&L during the session and acts when a rule is crossed.

Daily limits

Maximum loss on realized plus open P&L, a cap on entries per day, and a profit lock that protects a good session.

Position monitoring

Orders and positions sync from your broker every few seconds. If sync slows down, you see it straight away.

Risk alerts

Warnings before a limit is reached, alerts when it is crossed, and an audit trail of every action taken.

How it works

Three steps before your first session

STEP 1

Create your account

Sign up and turn on multi-factor authentication for sensitive actions.

STEP 2

Connect Dhan

Authorize through Dhan's own login. TradeLock never sees your broker password.

STEP 3

Set your rules

Pick your loss limit, trade cap, profit lock and cutoff time. Change them any time before the session.
